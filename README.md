# Agricultural Performance Dashboard (2024)

**Crop Yield, Soil Health, and Economic Impact Overview** — an interactive Tableau dashboard built on a global agriculture dataset from Kaggle.

[View the live dashboard on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/quudus.oladosu/viz/FinalProject_17921747813360/Dashboard3)

## What the dashboard shows

Six visualizations on one screen:

| Chart | What it answers |
|---|---|
| KPI cards | Top crop, top country, lowest soil health, highest economic impact at a glance |
| Countries by Crop Yield | How Australia, Brazil, China, France, Nigeria, and the USA compare against the global average |
| Net Yield Change per Crop (2024 vs 2023) | Which crops improved and which declined year over year |
| Adaptation Strategies Share | Breakdown of which farming adaptation methods are most commonly used |
| Fertilizer Usage vs Soil Health Index | Whether higher fertilizer use actually corresponds to better soil health by country |

## Key findings

- **Fruits had the highest yield gain** from 2023 to 2024 (+0.71 t/ha), while Wheat had the steepest decline (-0.50 t/ha).
- **Brazil topped country crop yield** at 2.36 t/ha, above the global average. Nigeria and the USA came in below average.
- **Australia had the highest economic impact** at $23,059M despite not having the highest yield — pointing to crop type and market value as bigger drivers than raw volume.
- **France had the lowest soil health index** (63.17) while also using relatively high fertilizer (50.8 kg/ha), suggesting fertilizer use alone does not protect soil health.
- **Over a quarter of farms (26.9%) used no adaptation strategy**, while drought-resistant crops (25.1%) were the most common adaptation method used.
